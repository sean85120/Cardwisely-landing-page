01 / How it works

From a stack of cards to tidy rows, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Line a card up inside the camera's guide frame, or pick photos you already have. Cardwisely reads the front and the back, in English and Chinese.

  2. 02

    Review

    It fills in name, company, title, phone, email, website and address. Contact QR codes fill in what the text missed. You review before saving, and you can tap a line of text to move it into the right field.

  3. 03

    Sort

    Put the card in a group and it joins the rest of your table. From the card you can call, email, or open the address in Maps.

02 / Sort and find

Built for the week after the conference.

Collecting cards is the easy part. These are the tools for finding the right person later.

No.FeatureWhat it does
  1. 01

    Groups

    Start with VCs, Partners, Potential clients, Might help in future and Not relevant now. Rename them, add your own, and put them in the order you want.

  2. 02

    Search and filter

    Search by name, company, email or phone. Filter by group, or by country or region.

  3. 03

    Bulk edits

    Select as many cards as you like, then move or delete them at once.

  4. 04

    Follow-ups

    See which new contacts are not in Contacts yet.

  5. 05

    Add to Contacts

    One tap opens the standard new-contact screen. You confirm the contact; the app never reads your address book.

  6. 06

    Export CSV

    Export all cards, the current results, or one group. The file opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.
