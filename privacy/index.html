Cardwisely Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. English first, 繁體中文 below.

跳至繁體中文

Summary

Cardwisely keeps your business cards on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any data about you or your cards. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

When you scan or add a card, the app saves the card's text fields, your notes, the group you chose, and the front and back photos in the app's private storage on your iPhone. This data is protected by iOS data protection and is removed when you delete the app or use Settings, then Delete all data. If iCloud sync is on, deleting the app does not remove your iCloud copies. Delete all data removes them while sync is on. If you turned sync off earlier, delete the copies in the iPhone Settings app: your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Cardwisely. If you back up your iPhone, the backup may include this data, under your own Apple account's backup settings.

iCloud sync (optional)

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, the app asks first, then keeps your cards and photos in your private iCloud so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot see, read or access it, and no server of ours is involved. While sync is on, Delete all data also removes cards from your other synced devices. If you turn sync off, copies already in iCloud stay there until you delete them.

Notion and Google Sheets (optional)

These are off by default and need the connect screen in Settings. If you connect one, the app first shows exactly what will be sent and asks for your consent. After you tap Sync now, the app copies your cards one way, straight from your iPhone to your own Notion workspace or to a spreadsheet it creates in your own Google Drive. What is sent: name, alternate name, company, title, phone numbers, email, website, address, area, country or region, group, the dates the card was added and changed, and your notes only if you switch on Include my notes. Photos of the card and the raw scanned text are never sent. We run no server for this, receive nothing, and cannot see your copy. The copy is under the terms and privacy policy of Notion or Google. For Google, the app can only access the spreadsheet it creates, and its use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to provide this feature and is not used for advertising or sold. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain, only on this device. Deleting a card in the app, or using Delete all data, does not delete the copy in Notion or Google: delete it there. To disconnect, use Settings, Notion and Google Sheets, Disconnect. This stops future sending and removes the token from your iPhone. To remove access completely, remove the integration in Notion (Settings, Connections) or Cardwisely at myaccount.google.com under Security, Third-party access.

Purchases

Cardwisely Pro is sold through the App Store, as a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app keeps a count of how many cards you have scanned in this iPhone's keychain, so it knows when the 10 free scans are used. The count never leaves the device.

How text is read

Text is recognized on your device with Apple's Vision framework. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, an Apple on-device language model helps tidy names, companies, titles and addresses. This also runs on your device: the model does not send card text to us or to Apple. Your cards are stored in your private iCloud only if you turn on sync, as described in the iCloud sync section.

Permissions

Camera: used only to photograph cards. Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose. Contacts: the app opens the system new-contact screen. It does not read your address book, and a contact is created only after you confirm it.

When data leaves the app

Only when you choose to: sharing a CSV file through the share sheet, saving a contact through the system new-contact screen, opening an address in Maps, or turning on iCloud sync, which stores your cards and photos in your private iCloud (Apple's service, under your Apple Account), or connecting Notion or Google Sheets and tapping Sync now, which copies your card fields straight to your own account there. Apart from iCloud sync and these two connections, what happens next is governed by the app you pick and its privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version moves card data to a service we run, or changes what the Notion and Google Sheets connections send, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first, and the feature will ask for your consent again.

Cardwisely 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日

摘要

Cardwisely 把你的名片保存在你的 iPhone 上；若你開啟 iCloud 同步，也會存放在你私人的 iCloud。我們不會收集、接收、販售或分享任何關於你或你名片的資料。沒有帳號、沒有我們的伺服器、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有追蹤。

App 會儲存什麼

當你掃描或新增名片時，App 會把名片的文字欄位、你的備註、你選的群組，以及正面和背面照片，儲存在 iPhone 上 App 專屬的儲存空間。這些資料受 iOS 資料保護，刪除 App 或在「設定」中使用「刪除所有資料」時會一併移除。若已開啟 iCloud 同步，刪除 App 不會移除 iCloud 上的副本；同步開啟時使用「刪除所有資料」會一併移除。如果你先前關閉了同步，請在 iPhone「設定」App 刪除副本：你的名字、iCloud、管理帳號儲存空間、Cardwisely。如果你備份 iPhone，備份可能包含這些資料，並依照你自己 Apple 帳號的備份設定處理。

購買

Cardwisely Pro 透過 App Store 銷售，可選擇月訂閱或一次買斷。付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會看到你的姓名、付款資料或 Apple 帳號。App 會在這支 iPhone 的鑰匙圈中記錄你掃描過幾張名片，用來判斷 10 次免費掃描是否用完。這個數字不會離開裝置。

文字如何被讀取

文字辨識使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架，在你的裝置上進行。支援 Apple 智慧功能的 iPhone，會用 Apple 的裝置端語言模型協助整理姓名、公司、職稱和地址，同樣在裝置上執行：該模型不會把名片文字傳給我們或 Apple。只有在你開啟同步時，名片才會存放在你私人的 iCloud，詳見「iCloud 同步」一節。

權限

相機：只用於拍攝名片。照片：App 使用系統照片挑選器，因此只能看到你選的照片。聯絡人：App 會開啟系統的新增聯絡人畫面，不會讀取你的通訊錄，而且要等你確認後才會建立聯絡人。

Notion 與 Google Sheets（選用）

預設為關閉，需要在「設定」的連線畫面操作。若你連接其中一項，App 會先清楚列出將傳送的內容並徵求你的同意。你點「立即同步」之後，App 會把名片單向複製，直接從你的 iPhone 傳到你自己的 Notion 工作區，或傳到它在你自己 Google 雲端硬碟中建立的試算表。傳送的內容：姓名、別名、公司、職稱、電話、電子郵件、網站、地址、地區、國家或地區、群組、名片新增與變更的日期，以及你的備註（僅在你開啟「包含我的備註」時）。名片照片和原始掃描文字絕不會被傳送。我們沒有為此運作伺服器，不會收到任何資料，也看不到你的副本。副本適用 Notion 或 Google 的條款與隱私權政策。就 Google 而言，App 只能存取它自己建立的試算表，且對 Google 資料的使用遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策（包含有限使用規定）：資料僅用於提供此功能，不會用於廣告或販售。登入權杖只保存在這支 iPhone 的鑰匙圈。在 App 中刪除名片或使用「刪除所有資料」，不會刪除 Notion 或 Google 中的副本，請在該處自行刪除。要中斷連線，請到「設定」→「Notion 與 Google Sheets」→「中斷連線」，這會停止之後的傳送並從 iPhone 移除權杖。若要完全移除存取權，請在 Notion（設定、連線）移除該整合，或到 myaccount.google.com 的「安全性」→「第三方存取」移除 Cardwisely。

iCloud 同步（選用）

同步預設為關閉。你在「設定」開啟時，App 會先詢問，然後把名片與照片存放在你私人的 iCloud，讓登入同一個 Apple 帳號的其他裝置也能看到。該儲存空間屬於你的 Apple 帳號，我們無法查看、讀取或存取，也沒有我們營運的伺服器參與。同步開啟時，「刪除所有資料」也會一併從你其他已同步的裝置移除名片。關閉同步後，已在 iCloud 上的副本會保留，直到你刪除。

資料何時會離開 App

只有在你主動選擇時：透過分享選單分享 CSV 檔案、透過系統新增聯絡人畫面儲存聯絡人、在地圖中開啟地址，或開啟 iCloud 同步，把名片和照片存放在你私人的 iCloud（Apple 的服務，屬於你的 Apple 帳號），或連接 Notion、Google Sheets 並點「立即同步」，把名片欄位直接複製到你在該處的帳號。除 iCloud 同步和這兩項連線外，之後的處理方式由你選擇的 App 及其隱私權政策決定。

兒童

本 App 不針對兒童，也不向任何人收集資料。

變更

如果未來版本會把名片資料送到我們營運的服務，或改變 Notion 與 Google Sheets 連線所傳送的內容，我們會先更新本政策和 App Store 隱私標籤，且該功能會再次徵求你的同意。
