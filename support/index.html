Contact

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: support@kairosaitech.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use. Do not send photos of cards that are not yours.

Common questions

The text on my card was read wrongly. Check the review screen before you save. Tap a line under "Unassigned text" to move it into a field, or edit a field by hand. Good light and a flat card help. Use the frame guide and fill it with the card.

The camera does not open. Open Settings, then Cardwisely, and turn on Camera. You can still use "Import from Photos" or "Add manually".

Why does Add to Contacts show a screen? Cardwisely opens the standard new-contact screen so you confirm each contact. The app does not read your existing contacts and does not check for duplicates there.

How do I get my cards into Excel or Google Sheets? Tap the menu, choose Export CSV, pick all cards, current results or one group, then share the file. CSV opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. Photos are not included.

How do I delete my data? Settings, then Delete all data. This removes every card and photo, restores the five default groups and disconnects Notion and Google Sheets (copies already sent stay in those accounts; delete them there). With iCloud sync on, cards are also removed from your other devices. If you turned sync off earlier, copies already in iCloud stay until you delete them in the iPhone Settings app: your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Cardwisely. Contacts you already added to your address book are kept.

Does it work offline? Yes. Everything runs on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, changes sync when you are next online.

How do I sync my cards between devices? Settings, then turn on Sync with iCloud on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account, then restart the app. Your cards and photos are kept in your private iCloud. Turning sync off later does not remove copies already in iCloud.

How do I send my cards to Notion or Google Sheets? Settings, then Notion and Google Sheets. Read what will be sent and tap Connect. For Notion, create an internal integration at notion.so/profile/integrations, paste its token, share one page with the integration (Connections on the page), then choose that page. For Google Sheets, sign in with Google; Cardwisely creates a spreadsheet called Cardwisely cards. Tap Sync now. Copying is one way and runs only when you tap. Photos and raw scanned text are never sent, and notes are sent only if you switch on Include my notes. If you connected before the Other Phone column was added, Cardwisely asks for your consent again; then disconnect and connect again so it creates a new database or spreadsheet with that column.

How do I disconnect Notion or Google Sheets? Settings, Notion and Google Sheets, Disconnect. Cards already copied stay in your account until you delete them there. To remove access fully, delete the integration in Notion, or remove Cardwisely at myaccount.google.com under Security, Third-party access.

Requirements. iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence improves name, company and title detection but is not required.

聯絡我們

問題、錯誤回報和功能建議請寄到 support@kairosaitech.com，並附上 iPhone 型號和 iOS 版本。請不要寄送不屬於你的名片照片。

常見問題

名片文字讀錯了。 儲存前請先檢查確認畫面。點「未指定的文字」下的一行文字，指定到欄位，或直接手動修改欄位。光線充足、名片平放，並讓名片填滿引導框，辨識會更準。

相機打不開。 請到「設定」，找到 Cardwisely，開啟相機權限。你仍然可以使用「從照片匯入」或「手動輸入」。

為什麼「加入聯絡人」會出現一個畫面？ Cardwisely 開啟系統的新增聯絡人畫面，讓你自己確認每位聯絡人。App 不會讀取你現有的聯絡人，也不會在其中檢查重複。

如何把名片放進 Excel 或 Google 試算表？ 點選單，選「匯出 CSV」，選擇全部名片、目前結果或單一群組，再分享檔案。CSV 可用 Excel、Numbers 和 Google 試算表開啟，不含照片。

如何刪除我的資料？ 到「設定」，點「刪除所有資料」。這會刪除所有名片和照片，並還原五個預設群組，也會中斷 Notion 與 Google Sheets 的連線（已傳送到那邊的副本不會被刪除，請在該處自行刪除）。開啟 iCloud 同步時，名片也會從你的其他裝置移除；如果你先前關閉了同步，iCloud 上的副本會保留，請到 iPhone「設定」App 刪除：你的名字、iCloud、管理帳號儲存空間、Cardwisely。已加入通訊錄的聯絡人會保留。

可以離線使用嗎？ 可以，所有處理都在你的 iPhone 上進行。如果你在「設定」開啟 iCloud 同步，變更會在下次連線時同步。

如何在多個裝置之間同步名片？ 在每個裝置的「設定」開啟「使用 iCloud 同步」（需登入同一個 Apple 帳號），然後重新啟動 App。名片與照片會存放在你私人的 iCloud。之後關閉同步，不會移除已在 iCloud 上的副本。

如何把名片傳到 Notion 或 Google Sheets？ 到「設定」→「Notion 與 Google Sheets」，閱讀將傳送的內容後點「連線」。Notion：到 notion.so/profile/integrations 建立內部整合，貼上權杖，把一個頁面分享給該整合（頁面的「連線」），然後選擇該頁面。Google Sheets：用 Google 登入，Cardwisely 會建立名為「Cardwisely 名片」的試算表。再點「立即同步」。複製是單向的，只有在你點的時候才會執行。照片和原始掃描文字絕不會被傳送，備註只有在你開啟「包含我的備註」時才會傳送。如果你在新增「其他電話」欄位之前就已連線，Cardwisely 會再次徵求你的同意；接著請中斷連線再重新連線，讓它建立含有該欄位的新資料庫或試算表。

如何中斷 Notion 或 Google Sheets 的連線？ 到「設定」→「Notion 與 Google Sheets」→「中斷連線」。已複製的名片會留在你的帳號，直到你在該處刪除。若要完全移除存取權，請在 Notion 刪除該整合，或到 myaccount.google.com 的「安全性」→「第三方存取」移除 Cardwisely。

系統需求。 iOS 26 或更新版本的 iPhone。支援 Apple 智慧功能時，姓名、公司和職稱的辨識會更好，但不是必要條件。
