Purchases and subscriptions

Cardwisely is free to download. The first 10 scanned cards are free, and adding cards by hand is always free.

Cardwisely Pro gives you unlimited scans. It is sold through the App Store in two ways: a monthly subscription at TWD 30 per month, or a one-time purchase of TWD 300 for life.

The monthly subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Apple handles the payment. To restore an earlier purchase, use Restore purchases in the app's Settings.
